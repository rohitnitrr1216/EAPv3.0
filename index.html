Dam Inundation – Submerged State, District & Village Finder

For Emergency Action Plan (EAP) preparation

  1. Click the box below and choose your inundation map (GeoTIFF).
  2. Wait for the result tables to appear (State/District table first, then the Village table).
  3. Download the tables as CSV (opens in Excel) and use them in the EAP.

How it works: every submerged pixel (value greater than 0) in the TIFF is checked against the District boundary, and the State comes from the District. Villages are then identified from the Survey of India village boundaries (only the State files that the inundation touches are loaded). Only the TIFF is needed; the boundaries are loaded automatically from the data folder.
